What Blurrd does

Blurrd is an Android video-editing tool published by Duck Devs that detects and masks faces in videos locally on your device. You can review detected face tracks, apply blur, mosaic, pixelate, or sticker masks, optionally save face signatures on your device to match across videos, and export the masked video.

Your responsibility

Automated face detection and matching are assistive tools and can miss faces, lose tracks, or misidentify people—especially in steep angles, heavy occlusion, rapid motion, dark lighting, or crowded scenes. Always inspect the entire exported video before sharing or publishing it. You are solely responsible for the videos you edit, the face information you choose to save, and ensuring you have the right to process and share that media.
