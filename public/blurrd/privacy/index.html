Blurrd Privacy Notice

Updated October 2026

Your media

Blurrd detects and masks faces on your device. Blurrd does not upload your videos, saved-face signatures, or thumbnails to a developer server. You choose whether to share an exported video with another app; that app then handles it under its own policy. Automatic detection can miss faces. Review the entire exported video before sharing it.

Local storage

The app stores projects, face signatures and thumbnails you choose to save, settings, a cached purchase status, and temporary export files in app storage. Importing a video creates a private cached copy so analysis and edits use consistent bytes. Queued exports keep a separate private copy until the export finishes or its abandoned job is cleaned up. Android may clear cached copies; the original video is never deleted by Blurrd. These are not application-level encrypted; Android's app sandbox and device security protect them. Blurrd disables app backup and requests exclusion from device transfers. Uninstalling the app or clearing its app storage removes this local data. Exported videos saved in your gallery remain until you delete them there. Original videos are not deleted. You can delete individual saved faces and projects within the app. Deleting a project removes its saved edits and its unshared cached video copy; an already queued export keeps its own source until it finishes or is cleaned up. Clearing Blurrd’s app storage removes all local projects, saved faces, settings, cached imports and queued export data.

Google services

Google ML Kit performs detection locally. Its SDK collects diagnostic and usage information, including device/app details, installation identifiers, performance, API configuration, and errors. Google documents HTTPS transport for this information. See https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Google Play handles purchases and payment information. Blurrd receives purchase state and a purchase token to acknowledge the Pro unlock. See https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/11416267.

Blurrd has no account system, advertising, or developer-operated analytics service. The third-party diagnostics above mean that a blanket “no data collected” claim would be inaccurate.

Permissions

You select videos through Android's system picker; the app does not request broad library access. On Android 7–9 only, saving an export to the shared gallery requires storage permission. Newer Android versions save through MediaStore. Foreground-service permissions allow an export to continue with an Android progress notification. Network access supports Google services; media analysis runs locally.

Audience and contact

Blurrd is intended as an editing tool for adults. Saving a face is optional. Only save face information you are entitled to use.
